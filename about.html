About

I am an AI Engineer from Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. I work on medical AI, with a focus on data that is far from perfect: few labels, many sources and many data types.

I studied Computer Science at Ho Chi Minh City University of Education. For my bachelor thesis, I used graph neural networks to predict diabetes from electronic health records. Since December 2025, I have been a research member at AI VIET NAM, where I work on semi- and weakly-supervised segmentation of histopathology images. This work led to papers at a MICCAI workshop and a CVPR workshop in 2026.

I also have industry experience. At GONSA, I built data pipelines for medical data, cleaned and matched drug data, and built a RAG system for drug package inserts. I have also taught Python and 3D design to students.

I am now looking for a research Master's position. If you work on medical AI with limited or mixed data, I would be glad to hear from you.
